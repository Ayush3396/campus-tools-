About Campus Exchange

A CA2 project built with Django, Python, and SQLite.

Problem Statement

College students regularly need items — scientific calculators, reference books, lab equipment, chargers, sports gear, and stationery — for a limited period of time. Purchasing these items solely for temporary use is expensive. At the same time, students who already own these items often leave them unused for extended periods.

There is no centralized, trusted platform where students can list items they're willing to lend or exchange, discover what's available on campus, and manage the entire lifecycle of a sharing transaction. Campus Exchange solves this.

Objectives
  • 1. Provide a single, trusted platform for students to list, discover, borrow, and exchange items on campus.
  • 2. Reduce unnecessary student expenditure by enabling borrowing instead of buying.
  • 3. Put idle items to productive use through a structured lending and exchange system.
  • 4. Implement a clear request lifecycle — from submission to approval to completion — with defined status stages.
  • 5. Demonstrate core Django concepts: ORM, forms, templates, authentication, sessions, and admin.
  • 6. Enforce security: ownership validation, permission checks, CSRF protection, and duplicate prevention.
How It Works

Lending

A student lists an item for Lending. Another student finds it on the Browse page, reads the details, and submits a borrowing request with a message and duration. The owner receives the request on their dashboard, and can approve or reject it. If approved, the item is marked as "Lent Out." After the item is returned, the owner marks the request as Completed and the item becomes available again.

Exchange

A student lists an item for Exchange. Another student proposes to swap their item in return — describing what they're offering. The owner reviews the proposal and accepts or rejects it. If accepted, both students arrange the physical exchange and the owner marks it as Completed.
